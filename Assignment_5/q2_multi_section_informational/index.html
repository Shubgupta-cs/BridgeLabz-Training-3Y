The Cultural Heart of Japan

Famous for its classical Buddhist temples, serene gardens, imperial palaces, and traditional wooden houses.

Historic Temples & Shrines

Explore Fushimi Inari Taisha with its thousands of vermilion torii gates and the golden pavilion of Kinkaku-ji.

Living Traditions

Experience authentic tea ceremonies, kaiseki dining, and stroll through the historic Gion district.

Best Time to Visit

Spring cherry blossoms (March–May) and autumn foliage (October–November) offer breathtaking scenery across the city.
